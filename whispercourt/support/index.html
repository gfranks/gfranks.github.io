Support

Questions, feedback, or stuck on a level? Email lgfz71@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Whisper Court has no servers and no account, and your progress is never sent anywhere.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

No. Because progress is stored only on the device, deleting the app deletes it too.

I found a bug, or have an idea

I'd like to hear about it — email lgfz71@gmail.com with as much detail as you can (which level, what you did, what you expected, what happened instead).
