Where is my data stored?

In your own private iCloud account, via Apple's CloudKit framework. Tydé has no servers of its own and the developer never has access to your data.

Do I need an account?

No. Tydé has no login and no account system. Just open the app and start using it — sync across your devices happens automatically if you're signed into iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. To also remove your synced iCloud data, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Tydé on your iPhone and delete it there, or email lgfz71@gmail.com and I can walk you through it.
