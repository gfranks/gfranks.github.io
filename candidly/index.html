About

Candidly connects to the Screen Sharing that's already built into macOS, so there's nothing to install on the Mac. Sign in with your Mac account, pick a display, and work with touch, a trackpad-style cursor, or a hardware keyboard and mouse. Your computers and their passwords follow you through iCloud.

What I built

  • The VNC (RFB) protocol written from scratch in Swift, including Apple's Screen Sharing extensions: Mac account sign-in over Diffie-Hellman and RSA, display selection, the shared-or-private session choice and the Mac clipboard. No third-party code.
  • ZRLE, Zlib, Hextile and CopyRect decoders hardened against a misbehaving server, with output and size limits, and tested against an in-process fake server and a real VNC server.
  • A Metal renderer that uploads only the parts of the screen that changed, so a 5K Mac stays smooth on an iPhone.
  • Touch and Trackpad modes with the full gesture set: multi-finger clicks and drags, two-finger scroll, pinch zoom, edge and corner swipes, and three-finger swipes for Mission Control and Spaces.
  • Saved computers sync through iCloud with merge rules and deletion tombstones, passwords live in iCloud Keychain, and Macs on the network are found with Bonjour.
