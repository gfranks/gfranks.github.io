About

Candidly connects to the Screen Sharing that's already built into macOS, so there's nothing to install on the Mac. Use your phone as the trackpad and keyboard, float your Mac in Picture in Picture while you use other apps, get a notification when a build or export finishes, copy text straight off the screen, keep up to four Macs open, and wake a sleeping one with a tap. Your computers and their passwords follow you through iCloud. One price, no subscription.

What I built

01

The VNC (RFB) protocol written from scratch in Swift, including Apple's Screen Sharing extensions: Mac account sign-in over Diffie-Hellman and RSA, display selection, the shared-or-private session choice, the Mac clipboard and curtain mode. No third-party code.

02

Whole-session encryption with Apple's own Screen Sharing scheme (AES-128 records, on by default after Mac account sign-in), a lock in the viewer, and a per-computer setting that refuses unencrypted sessions.

03

A Metal renderer that uploads only the parts of the screen that changed, so a 5K Mac stays smooth on an iPhone, plus ZRLE, Zlib, Hextile and CopyRect decoders hardened against a misbehaving server.

04

Picture in Picture for a remote screen: frames scaled with vImage into an AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer, a session that stays live in the background while PiP is open, and a handover when you switch Macs or windows.

05

Watch Area: draw a box over the Mac's screen and get a notification when it changes or stops changing, using a luminance grid that ignores a blinking cursor but catches a slow progress bar.

06

Up to four Macs open at once with a switcher, and multiple windows on iPad, built on an app-wide session manager that pauses whatever isn't on screen.

07

Select Text with Live Text (VisionKit) on a full-resolution crop of what you're looking at, Wake-on-LAN with an on-demand check for Macs that aren't responding, Trackpad Only mode, and custom shortcut buttons on the key strip.

08

Touch and Trackpad modes with the full gesture set (multi-finger clicks and drags, two-finger scroll, pinch zoom at your fingers, edge and corner swipes, three-finger swipes for Mission Control and Spaces) and the Mac's real pointer drawn on the device.

09

Saved computers sync through iCloud with merge rules and deletion tombstones, passwords live in iCloud Keychain, and Macs on the network are found with Bonjour.
