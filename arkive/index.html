About

Read a verse per page or by chapter, tap any word for the original language, and follow it through the whole Bible. Commentaries, dictionaries, a timeline and journey maps sit behind the text, and your notes and study threads sync through your own iCloud.

What I built

01

Three SwiftData stores in one container: bundled Bible text, a purgeable cache for online text, and CloudKit-synced user data with merge rules for records two devices create at once.

02

Full-text and grammar search over a prebuilt SQLite FTS5 index, including 440,000 original-language words searchable by Strong's number, tense and voice.

03

A licensed translation fetched live from the YouVersion API and transcoded from HTML into the app's own text format, so every reader feature works on it unchanged.

04

Read to Me: on-device speech with an IPA pronunciation lexicon for biblical names, a Live Activity, a sleep timer and Personal Voice support.

05

MapKit timelines and animated journey routes, plus a deep-zoom 1878 history chart drawn with CATiledLayer and tappable scenes.

06

Python data pipelines that turn public-domain lexicons, dictionaries and commentaries into validated bundles, with license checks built in.

07

Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight, Handoff, hardware keyboard shortcuts and the iPad menu bar.
