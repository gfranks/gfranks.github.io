Whisper Court ("the app") is a strategy game developed by Garrett Franks. This policy describes what data the app does and does not collect.

Data We Collect

None. Whisper Court does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer or any third party.

Game Progress

Your progress, such as which levels you have completed and the candles you have earned, is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network Use

All levels, artwork, and game content are bundled within the app. The app makes no network requests to fetch or transmit content.

In-App Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics & Advertising

Whisper Court contains no analytics, tracking SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Whisper Court does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
