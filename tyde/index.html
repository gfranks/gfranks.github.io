About

Every task is sorted Easy, Medium or Deep, so a low-energy day still has a real session in it. Pick up to three must-dos, and spin the wheel when you can't decide where to start.

  • Swift
  • SwiftUI

  • App Intents
  • Lottie

What I built

  • SwiftData with CloudKit sync for rooms, tasks, plans and history, with no account and no server.
  • A Home Screen widget that shows today's room and can spin for a task without opening the app, plus a Live Activity for the focus timer, sharing data through an App Group.
  • Siri and Shortcuts through App Intents: spin for a task or hear today's room hands-free.
  • Lottie animations for the moments that matter, and copy written to avoid streak-shaming or guilt.
